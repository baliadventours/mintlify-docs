Your Tripbone store is the digital storefront guests see when they browse and book your tours. Before you publish your first tour, spend a few minutes setting up your brand, domain, currency, and contact details so every page looks professional and builds trust with travelers.

## Open Global Settings

1. Log in to your Tripbone dashboard.
2. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
3. Select **Store Setup** from the top tab row.

## Upload your brand assets

Tripbone supports three logo variants so your brand looks sharp in every context.

1. Under **Brand Assets**, click the upload area for each variant:
   - **Primary logo** — used on light backgrounds
   - **Dark mode logo** — used when the site switches to dark theme
   - **Favicon** — the small icon shown in browser tabs (square, 512 x 512 px recommended)
2. Drag and drop your images or click to browse.
3. Click **Save** after each upload.

## Set brand colors and theme

1. In the **Theme** section, pick a base color that matches your brand.
2. Tripbone generates a full accessible palette automatically (primary, accent, text, and background shades).
3. Toggle **Dark mode support** if you want the site to follow the visitor's system preference.
4. Click **Apply Theme**.

## Choose currency and date format

1. Under **Regional Settings**, select your default **Currency** from the dropdown (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, and 40\+ others).
2. Pick a **Date format** (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Choose your **Time format** (12-hour or 24-hour).
4. Click **Save**.

These settings control how prices and dates appear on your storefront and in booking confirmations.

## Map a custom domain

You can run your store on your own subdomain, such as `tours.yourbrand.com`, so guests never leave your brand experience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    In the **Custom Domain** section, type your desired subdomain (for example, `tours.yourbrand.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS record">
    Tripbone shows a CNAME value. Open your domain registrar or DNS provider and create a CNAME record pointing your subdomain to the value shown in Tripbone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in Tripbone">
    Return to Tripbone and click **Verify Domain**. Propagation usually takes a few minutes, but can take up to 24 hours depending on your DNS provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as primary">
    Once verified, toggle **Use as primary domain** so all store links redirect to your custom domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  SSL is included for free on all custom domains. Tripbone provisions and renews the certificate automatically; you do not need to upload or manage anything.
</Note>

## Set WhatsApp and support contacts

1. Under **Contact & Messaging**, enter your business **WhatsApp number** (with country code, e.g., \+1-555-123-4567).
2. Add your **Support email** address.
3. Enter your **Business phone** and **Physical address** if you want them displayed on the storefront.
4. Click **Save**.

The WhatsApp number powers the built-in dispatch system, so guides and drivers can receive booking details directly in WhatsApp.

## Connect social profiles

1. Scroll to **Social Links**.
2. Paste the URLs for your active profiles:
   - Instagram
   - TikTok
   - TripAdvisor
   - Facebook
   - YouTube
3. Toggle **Show on storefront** for each profile you want visible to guests.
4. Click **Save**.
