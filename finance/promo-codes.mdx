Promo codes and last-minute deals are two of the most effective ways to increase occupancy and attract new guests. Tripbone lets you create flexible discount codes in seconds, and also mark specific departures as On Sale when you need to fill empty seats before a tour leaves.

## Create a promo code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Promo Codes">
    Navigate to **Finance \> Promo Codes** and click **Create Promo Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your code">
    Enter a code guests will type at checkout, such as `SUMMER20` or `WELCOME10`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose discount type">
    Select **Percentage off** (e.g., 20% off the total) or **Fixed amount off** (e.g., \$15 off).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set rules">
    Configure any combination of the following:

    - **Expiry date**: The code stops working after this date.
    - **Minimum spend**: The booking total must exceed this amount for the code to apply.
    - **First booking only**: Restrict the code to guests who have never booked with you before.
    - **Usage limit**: Cap the total number of times the code can be redeemed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Click **Save**. The code is active immediately. Share it via email, social media, or WhatsApp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark a departure as On Sale

When a tour date is approaching and seats are still available, you can drop the price directly on the departure without creating a code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the departure">
    Go to **Inventory \> Availability** and locate the date you want to promote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable On Sale">
    Click the departure and toggle **On Sale**. Enter the new price or discount percentage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sale window">
    Optionally set a **Sale ends** time so the discount automatically expires before departure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Guests browsing your storefront will see the original price struck through and the sale price highlighted. No code is required at checkout.

## Log refunds for cancellations

When a guest cancels and you process a refund, log it in Tripbone so your profit dashboard stays accurate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking">
    Go to **Bookings \> Manage**, find the booking, and click **View Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log refund">
    Click **Log Refund**, enter the refunded amount, and select the reason (guest cancellation, operator cancellation, force majeure, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review impact">
    The refund is subtracted from your gross revenue in the profit dashboard, and any associated commission is recalculated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Refund logging is for accounting transparency. The actual money transfer happens through your payment gateway. Tripbone tracks the financial record so your reports stay accurate.
</Note>
