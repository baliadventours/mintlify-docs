If you work with local guides, drivers, or third-party tour suppliers, Tripbone makes it easy to track exactly what you owe and when. You can set commission rules per vendor or per tour, then let the system calculate payouts automatically as bookings come in.

## Set commission split rules

Commission rules determine how much of each booking goes to the vendor and how much stays with your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vendor Commissions">
    Go to **Finance \> Commissions** and click **Add Vendor** or select an existing vendor from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the split type">
    Select either **Percentage of booking total** (e.g., 30% to the guide) or **Fixed amount per guest** (e.g., \$15 per person).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply scope">
    Choose whether the rule applies to **all tours** or **specific tours only**. This is useful when some vendors supply only one experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save Rule**. The commission will now be calculated automatically on every new booking that matches the scope.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track payouts

The Payout Tracker shows every commission owed to your vendors, split into two tabs:

- **Pending**: Commissions calculated from confirmed bookings that have not yet been paid.
- **Paid**: Commissions you have marked as settled, with the date and method recorded.

To mark a payout as paid, select the vendor, check the bookings you want to settle, and click **Mark as Paid**. You can record the payment method (bank transfer, cash, etc.) and add a reference number for your records.

## Add taxes and fees as line items

For transparent accounting, you can add VAT, service charges, or booking fees as separate line items on every invoice and commission report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fee Configuration">
    In **Finance \> Commissions**, click **Configure Fees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a fee type">
    Click **Add Fee** and enter the name (e.g., "VAT 10%", "Service Charge", "Booking Fee").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set calculation method">
    Choose whether the fee is a **percentage** of the subtotal or a **fixed amount** per booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Display on invoices">
    Toggle **Show on Guest Invoice** and **Show on Vendor Report** so both parties see the breakdown clearly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automated commission calculations

Tripbone recalculates commissions in real time when:

- A new booking is confirmed
- A guest modifies their party size
- A refund or partial refund is processed
- A promo code is applied retroactively

You do not need to update spreadsheets manually. The commission report always reflects the current state of your bookings.

<Tip>
  Use the **Occupancy Yield** report in your dashboard to spot underperforming tours. If a tour consistently runs below capacity, consider renegotiating the vendor commission or promoting it with a targeted discount.
</Tip>
