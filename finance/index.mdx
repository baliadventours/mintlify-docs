Tripbone's finance module is built to give tour operators complete visibility into their revenue without the complexity of traditional accounting software. Because Tripbone charges zero booking fees, every dollar that flows through your store is yours to track, split, and report on in real time.

The finance area covers four core functions: a profit dashboard that updates with every booking, vendor commission tracking for partners and guides, promo code and discount management, and automated PDF invoicing for your guests.

## What you can do in Finance

- **Profit Dashboard**: See gross revenue, net profit, refunds, and commissions owed in one live view.
- **Vendor Payouts**: Configure commission splits per vendor or per tour, then track what is pending versus paid.
- **Promo Codes and Discounts**: Create percentage or fixed-amount codes, set expiry dates, and run last-minute seat sales.
- **Automated Invoicing**: Generate branded PDF invoices for every booking with line-item breakdowns including VAT and service fees.

## How profit is calculated

Tripbone calculates net profit automatically for every booking using this formula:

```text
Net Profit = Gross Revenue - Vendor Commissions - Refunds - Promo Discounts
```

The profit dashboard updates in real time as bookings are created, modified, or cancelled. You can filter by date range, tour, or vendor to drill into specific performance.
