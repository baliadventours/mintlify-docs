The Settings panel is your control center for everything that runs behind the scenes in Tripbone. From team permissions to third-party integrations and disaster recovery, this is where you configure the rules that govern how your store operates.

## What Settings covers

- **Brand and Design**: Colors, fonts, logo, and storefront layout. These are managed in the [Design](/design) section for a visual editing experience.
- **Core Preferences**: Currency, timezone, language, booking cutoff times, and cancellation policies.
- **User and Team Management**: Invite staff, assign roles, and control who can see finances, bookings, or inventory.
- **Integrations and Developer Tools**: Connect analytics, webhooks, live chat widgets, and API access.
- **Backup and Restore**: Take a full snapshot of your database before major changes, and roll back instantly if needed.

## Quick settings reference

| Setting | Where to find it | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Currency and timezone | Settings \> General | Super Admin, Operations Manager |
| Booking cutoff rules | Settings \> Bookings | Super Admin, Operations Manager |
| Cancellation policy text | Settings \> Bookings | Super Admin, Operations Manager |
| Team invites | Settings \> Users | Super Admin |
| Backup and restore | Settings \> System | Super Admin |
| Analytics scripts | Settings \> Integrations | Super Admin, Operations Manager |
