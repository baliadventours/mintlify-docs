Tripbone's blog engine helps you publish travel content that ranks on Google and drives direct bookings. It suggests topics based on your live inventory, generates full 800\+ word SEO articles, and inserts internal links to your tours automatically. You can edit, publish, and promote a new post in minutes instead of hours.

## How the topic engine works

The blog engine scans your inventory and upcoming local events to suggest article topics that match what travelers are searching for. For example, if you offer a sunrise volcano hike, it might suggest "Best Sunrise Hikes in Bali for Solo Travelers." Each suggested topic includes a target keyword and estimated search volume.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Blog Engine">
    Go to **Content \> Blog Engine** in your Tripbone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review suggested topics">
    Browse the list of AI-suggested topics. Each card shows the target keyword, estimated search volume, and which of your tours will be linked inside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the article">
    Click **Generate** on a topic you like. The AI writes a full 800\+ word article with headings, local tips, and internal links to your tours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and publish">
    Review the draft, adjust any details, and click **Publish**. The post goes live on your blog with an auto-generated thumbnail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review sentiment integration

The blog engine can read your guest reviews and update "Why Choose Us" sections automatically. It pulls the most common positive themes from recent feedback and turns them into persuasive copy within the article. This keeps your content grounded in real guest experiences.

## Auto-generated blog thumbnails

Every published post receives an AI-generated thumbnail based on the topic and your brand colors. You can replace it with a custom image at any time from the post editor.

<Tip>
  Publish one blog post per week to build topical authority. The blog engine will suggest seasonal topics automatically, such as "Best Rainy Season Activities" or "Holiday Market Tours."
</Tip>
