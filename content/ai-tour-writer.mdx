The AI Tour Architect turns a simple list of highlights into a complete, professional tour listing. Instead of staring at a blank page, you enter a few bullet points and the AI generates a compelling title, SEO-friendly description, time-stamped itinerary, and a practical packing list. This saves hours of writing time and keeps your listings consistent.

## Generate a tour description

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tour in Inventory">
    Navigate to **Inventory \> Tours** and open the tour you want to enhance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Generate with AI'">
    Find the AI Tour Architect button in the tour editor panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your highlights">
    Type a short list of what the tour includes. For example: `Waterfall, Swing, Coffee Plantation, Local Lunch, Guide`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tone">
    Select **Adventurous**, **Luxurious**, or **Family-Friendly** to match your target guest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    The AI generates a full draft. Edit any details, then click **Save and Publish** to update your live tour page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the AI generates

- **Professional title** — Optimized for search and click-through rate.
- **SEO description** — A concise summary that appears in Google search results.
- **Time-stamped itinerary** — A hour-by-hour breakdown of the experience.
- **What to Bring list** — Practical items based on the activity type and location.

<Tip>
  Use the sentiment analysis feature to pull real selling points from guest reviews. The AI scans your review feed and highlights phrases guests use most often, such as "amazing guide" or "breathtaking views," then weaves them into the generated description.
</Tip>
