Tripbone is built on a simple philosophy: you should own your tools, not rent them. Every plan is a one-time purchase. There are no monthly fees, no per-booking commissions, and no surprise charges. You pay once, and the system is yours forever.

## Tripbone vs. typical tour software

| Feature | Tripbone | Typical SaaS (Rezdy, FareHarbor, etc.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly fee** | \$0 | $$99 - $$499 per month |
| **Booking fees** | 0% | 1% - 6% per transaction |
| **AI itinerary planner** | Included | Expensive add-on or not available |
| **Ownership** | You own it | You rent it |
| **Domain mapping** | Included (System \+ Installation and above) | Often extra |
| **Vendor commission tracking** | Built in | Often requires upgrade |
| **Support model** | Direct line (System \+ Installation and above) | Tiered, slower on lower plans |

## What is included in every plan

No matter which plan you choose, you get:

- Zero booking fees on every transaction
- Google Gemini AI for tour writing, itinerary planning, and OTA data extraction
- Firebase-powered hosting with global CDN and automatic scaling
- Unlimited tours, bookings, and guests
- The Bento Box drag-and-drop page builder
- Multi-role user management
- 1-click backup and restore

## Which plan is right for you?

- **System Only** is ideal if you are comfortable with DNS, hosting, and basic server configuration. You get the full product at the lowest price.
- **System \+ Installation** is the best fit for most operators. You get hands-on help with domain setup, tour migration, and WhatsApp integration, plus a direct support line for questions.
- **Enterprise** is designed for agencies, multi-brand operators, or anyone who needs custom design, API access, and a dedicated partner to optimize the platform.

<Note>
  There are zero recurring fees on all plans. The one-time price is the total cost of ownership. You only pay your payment gateway's standard processing fees, which apply to any online business.
</Note>
