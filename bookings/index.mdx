Tripbone's booking dashboard is your single source of truth for every reservation that comes in, whether from your website, an OTA, or a walk-in guest. You can track the full lifecycle of each booking, assign guides and drivers, and keep guests informed — all without paying any booking fees.

## Booking lifecycle

Every booking moves through a clear set of stages so your team always knows what is happening next.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The guest has submitted a request or started checkout. Payment or confirmation may still be needed. |
| **Confirmed** | The booking is locked in. The guest has paid or you have manually confirmed the reservation. |
| **Picking Up** | The guide or driver is en route to the pickup location. This stage triggers dispatch messages automatically. |
| **In Progress** | The tour is currently running. Use this status to prevent double-booking the same guide or vehicle. |
| **Completed** | The tour has finished. The booking is closed and ready for review, commission calculation, or follow-up. |

You can move a booking between statuses manually, or let Tripbone advance it automatically based on the tour schedule and dispatch confirmations.

## What you can do in Bookings

- **Import OTA reservations** — Paste a Viator, Klook, or GetYourGuide confirmation email and Tripbone extracts the guest data instantly.
- **Manage and update bookings** — Search, filter, edit details, and send WhatsApp confirmations from one screen.
- **Dispatch your team** — Assign guides and drivers to departures, send WhatsApp manifests, and export PDF pickup lists.
