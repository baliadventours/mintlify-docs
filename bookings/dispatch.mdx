Dispatch is how you get the right people to the right place at the right time. In Tripbone, you assign guides and drivers to each departure, send them a WhatsApp message with the full guest list and pickup details, and export a PDF manifest for offline use. Everything is tied to the booking status, so your team moves from Confirmed to Picking Up to In Progress without confusion.

## Assign a guide or driver to a departure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the departure">
    In your Tripbone admin, go to **Bookings** \> **Dispatch**. You will see a list of today's and upcoming departures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the tour and time slot">
    Click the departure row for the tour and time you want to staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign staff">
    In the detail panel, use the **Guide** and **Driver** dropdowns to select from your team list. If the person is not listed yet, add them in **Settings** \> **User Roles** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignment">
    Click **Update Dispatch**. The assigned guide and driver are now linked to every booking on that departure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A guide or driver cannot be double-booked for overlapping departures. Tripbone warns you if you try to assign someone who is already scheduled elsewhere.
</Note>

## Send a WhatsApp dispatch message

Once a guide or driver is assigned, you can send them a WhatsApp message with all the details they need for the departure.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the departure in Dispatch">
    Go to **Bookings** \> **Dispatch** and click the departure row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the manifest">
    The panel shows the guest list, pickup locations, number of travelers, and any special requests (dietary needs, mobility assistance, child seats).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send WhatsApp dispatch">
    Click **Send WhatsApp Dispatch**. Tripbone sends a formatted message to the assigned guide and driver with the guest manifest, pickup order, and meeting point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The WhatsApp message includes:

- Tour name and departure time
- Guest names and contact numbers
- Pickup locations in route order
- Number of adults, children, and infants
- Special requests and notes

## Export a PDF guest manifest

For airport pickups, boat departures, or areas with poor mobile signal, print a PDF manifest before you leave.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the departure">
    In **Bookings** \> **Dispatch**, click the departure you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the manifest">
    Click **Export PDF Manifest**. Tripbone creates a branded PDF with the guest list, pickup order, and emergency contact numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or print">
    Open the PDF and print copies for your guide, driver, and checkpoint staff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Group pickups by zone

If you run hotel pickups across a large area, grouping stops by zone helps drivers take the shortest route and avoid backtracking.

<Tip>
  Group pickups by zone, such as Kuta/Seminyak Zone, Ubud Zone, or Nusa Dua Zone. In the dispatch panel, Tripbone sorts pickup locations within each zone so your driver can follow a logical route. You can label zones in your tour settings under **Pickup Locations**.
</Tip>

To set up zones, go to **Inventory** \> select your tour \> **Pickup Locations** and assign each hotel or address to a zone name. The dispatch manifest will then group and sort by zone automatically.
