The booking dashboard is where you handle every reservation that comes into Tripbone. You can search across all bookings, update details on the fly, log walk-in guests who pay cash, and send WhatsApp confirmations without leaving the page.

## Filter and search bookings

Use the filters at the top of the dashboard to narrow down the list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking dashboard">
    In your Tripbone admin, go to **Bookings** \> **Manage Bookings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the dropdowns to filter by **Status** (Pending, Confirmed, Picking Up, In Progress, Completed), **Tour**, **Date range**, or **Guest name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type a guest name, booking reference, or phone number into the search bar to find a specific reservation instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list updates in real time as you type or change filters. Click any row to open the full booking detail panel.

## Update a booking status

When a guest pays, cancels, or boards the tour, keep the record accurate so your team stays coordinated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking">
    Click the booking row in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the status">
    In the detail panel, click the **Status** dropdown and select the new stage: Pending, Confirmed, Picking Up, In Progress, or Completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click **Update Booking**. The guest receives an automatic status update if you have WhatsApp notifications enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Marking a booking as **Completed** locks it from further edits. Make sure all payments and notes are finalized before you close it.
</Warning>

## Add a manual booking (walk-in or WhatsApp)

Not every guest books through your website. Use Quick-Book to log reservations that arrive by phone, WhatsApp, or in person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quick-Book">
    In the booking dashboard, click **Quick-Book** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter guest details">
    Fill in the guest name, phone number, email, and number of travelers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the tour and date">
    Choose the tour from your inventory, then pick the date and available time slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payment method">
    Choose **Cash**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Pending** if payment is still due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the booking">
    Click **Create Booking**. The reservation appears in your dashboard immediately and a confirmation message is sent if WhatsApp is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share the Booking Tracker with guests

Every booking in Tripbone gets a unique Booking Tracker link. Guests can open it on their phone to see their tour status, pickup time, and meeting point — without calling your office.

<Note>
  The Booking Tracker is a unique URL per booking. Share it via WhatsApp or email so guests can self-serve their own trip details. This reduces repetitive "What time is pickup?" messages to your team.
</Note>

To find the link, open any booking and copy the **Booking Tracker URL** from the detail panel.

## Generate a PDF invoice

You can create and download a branded PDF invoice for any confirmed or completed booking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking">
    Click the booking row in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the invoice">
    In the detail panel, click **Generate Invoice**. Tripbone fills in guest details, tour name, date, and total price automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or send">
    Preview the PDF, then click **Download** or **Send to Guest** to deliver it by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
