This guide takes you from purchase to your first live booking in about 20 minutes. You will access your dashboard, configure your brand, add a tour, set pricing and availability, and then take a test booking to confirm everything works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your dashboard after purchase">
    After you complete your purchase, check your email for a welcome message with your Tripbone dashboard link and login credentials. Click the link to open your dashboard and sign in. You will land on the main dashboard where you can see your store overview, recent bookings, and quick-action tiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure brand settings">
    Open **Settings \> Brand** from the sidebar. Upload your logo, choose your primary brand color, and set your store name and default currency. If you already own a domain, go to **Settings \> Domain** and enter your custom domain (for example, `tours.yourbrand.com`). Tripbone will generate DNS records for you to add at your registrar. These settings control how your storefront looks to guests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first tour using the inventory builder">
    Go to **Inventory \> Tours** and click **Add Tour**. Enter the tour name, duration, meeting point, and a short description. Upload a cover image and any additional gallery photos. Set the maximum group size and choose whether guests can book instantly or if you require manual confirmation. Save the tour when you are done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set pricing and availability">
    Inside your new tour, open the **Pricing** tab. Add at least one pricing tier, such as "Adult" with your standard rate. Then open the **Availability** tab and add time slots for the days you want to accept bookings. You can create repeating weekly schedules or add one-off dates for special events. Make sure at least one slot is marked as available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your store link and take a test booking">
    Click **Preview Store** in the top navigation to open your live storefront. Copy the URL and paste it into a new browser tab or send it to your phone. Select your tour, pick an available date and time, and complete the checkout flow using a test payment method or the manual-payment option. Check your dashboard under **Bookings** to confirm the reservation appears with the correct guest details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a personal email address for your test booking so you can see the confirmation email and booking tracker link exactly as your guests will.
</Tip>

Once your test booking is confirmed, your store is live and ready for real guests. From here, you can expand your inventory, connect a payment processor, and turn on AI content generation.
