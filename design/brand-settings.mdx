Tripbone's Brand Settings panel gives you centralized control over your storefront's visual identity. Upload your logo, set a global color palette, choose font pairings, and manage your favicon from one place. Every change applies instantly across your entire site, including tour pages, blog posts, and the booking checkout.

## Configure your brand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Brand">
    Open the Brand Settings panel from your Tripbone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logos">
    Add your primary logo for light backgrounds and a dark-mode variant for night-themed pages or dark UI sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your favicon">
    Upload a square PNG or ICO file. This appears in browser tabs and bookmark icons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a global color palette">
    Click the color swatch panel to choose a preset palette or define custom hex values for primary, secondary, background, and text colors. The Global Palette Swap updates every component on your site instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font pairing">
    Browse the font library and select a heading font and a body font. All pages will render with your chosen pair.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Use the live preview to see how your brand looks on a tour page and the homepage. Click **Save** when you are satisfied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Logo best practices

- Use a transparent PNG for the primary logo so it blends cleanly with any background.
- Keep the dark-mode logo visible against dark backgrounds, usually by inverting colors or adding a light outline.
- Favicon files should be at least 32x32 pixels for crisp display on retina screens.

## Font pairings

The font library includes curated pairings optimized for travel and hospitality brands. Pairings are grouped by mood:

| Pairing style | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Modern Sans | Clean, minimal storefronts |
| Elegant Serif | Luxury and private tour brands |
| Playful Display | Family-friendly and adventure operators |

<Note>
  Enterprise plans can inject custom CSS overrides for advanced styling beyond the standard palette and font options. Contact support to enable custom CSS on your account.
</Note>
