The Bento Box Builder is Tripbone's modular page editor. It lets you assemble high-conversion storefront pages by dragging content blocks into place. No coding is required. Each module is pre-designed for travel businesses, so you spend less time styling and more time selling tours.

## Available modules

The builder includes modules designed specifically for tour operators:

| Module | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Tour Grids | Display your tours in a responsive grid with filters and booking buttons. |
| Testimonial Sliders | Rotate guest reviews with star ratings and photos to build trust. |
| AI Planner Prompts | Embed the AItinerary interview directly on any page to capture leads. |
| Video Backgrounds | Add full-width looping video behind headlines or CTAs. |
| FAQ Accordions | Answer common guest questions in a collapsible format. |

## Build a page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Bento Builder">
    Navigate to **Design \> Bento Builder** and select the page you want to edit, or click **New Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag modules onto the canvas">
    Browse the module sidebar and drag any block onto your page. Drop it above, below, or between existing modules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each module">
    Click a module to open its settings panel. Choose which tours to display, select a video file, or customize the AI planner prompt text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on mobile">
    Toggle the mobile preview to ensure your layout looks correct on phones. Most travel bookings happen on mobile, so this step is essential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or stage">
    Click **Publish** to go live immediately, or **Save as Draft** to stage changes and swap them in later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Safe staging

Work on a new version of any page without affecting your live site. The Bento Builder saves draft versions separately. When you are ready, swap the draft to live with one click. This is useful for seasonal campaigns or promotional updates.

## Performance grading

Every page receives a performance score based on load speed, image optimization, and mobile responsiveness. The builder highlights specific fixes, such as compressing a video or resizing an image, so you can keep your storefront fast.

<Tip>
  Try the AI Layout Suggestions feature. It analyzes your inventory and recommends a page layout optimized for conversion, such as placing your highest-reviewed tour in the top grid slot.
</Tip>
