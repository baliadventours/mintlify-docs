Tripbone's Design Builder lets you create a professional travel storefront without writing code. It combines a modular page builder with centralized brand controls, so you can drag and drop content blocks, adjust your visual identity, and launch a polished site in a single afternoon.

The system is built around two core tools: the Bento Box Builder for page layout, and Brand Settings for global color, font, and logo control. Changes in Brand Settings apply instantly across every page on your site.

## What the design suite covers

- **Bento Box Builder** — Drag and drop tour grids, review sliders, AI planner prompts, video backgrounds, and FAQ accordions to build high-conversion pages.
- **Brand Settings** — Upload your logo, set a global color palette, choose font pairings, and manage your favicon from one panel.
