AItinerary is Tripbone's built-in AI travel planner powered by Google Gemini. It interviews your guests about their travel vibe, budget, and interests, then builds a custom day-by-day itinerary that weaves your own tours directly into the plan. Every recommendation includes a direct booking link back to your store, turning research into revenue.

Research fatigue is the number one cause of booking abandonment. Guests browse dozens of tabs, compare options, and leave without booking. AItinerary solves this by doing the research for them in a single conversational session. Guests answer a few questions, and the AI handles the rest.

## What AItinerary does

- **Conversational interview** — Guests share their travel dates, budget, group size, and interests through a friendly chat interface.
- **Inventory matching** — The AI prioritizes your own tours and experiences first, then fills gaps with relevant local recommendations.
- **Direct booking links** — Every tour slot in the itinerary links straight to your booking page, so guests can reserve instantly.
- **Collaborative sharing** — Guests can share their plan with travel companions via a unique link, driving repeat traffic to your site.

## How it helps your business

AItinerary captures guests earlier in the booking funnel, before they visit an OTA. By owning the planning experience, you keep the full booking value instead of paying 15-30% commission to a marketplace. The planner also generates high-intent leads when guests save their itinerary by sharing an email or WhatsApp number.
