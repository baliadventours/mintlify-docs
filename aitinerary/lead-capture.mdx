AItinerary does not just help guests plan trips. It also captures high-intent leads before they reach the checkout page. When a guest builds a personalized itinerary, they are asked to save it by sharing an email address or WhatsApp number. This simple step turns anonymous browsers into contacts you can follow up with.

## How the save prompt works

After the AI generates a day-by-day itinerary, the guest sees a save prompt. To keep their plan, they must enter an email address or WhatsApp number. Once they submit, the itinerary is saved to their profile and a shareable link is created.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Guest completes the interview">
    The guest answers questions about dates, budget, vibe, and group size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI generates the itinerary">
    Google Gemini builds a custom plan featuring your tours with direct booking links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save prompt appears">
    The guest is asked to enter an email or WhatsApp number to save and share the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lead is captured">
    The contact is added to your admin lead feed with a link to their full itinerary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View leads in your admin feed

Open **AItinerary \> Leads** in your Tripbone dashboard to see every captured contact. Each entry shows:

- Contact method (email or WhatsApp)
- Date captured
- Link to the guest's generated itinerary
- Estimated trip dates and group size

Use this feed to follow up with personalized offers, answer questions, or nudge guests toward booking.

## Collaborative share links drive repeat traffic

Every saved itinerary gets a unique share link. Guests can send it to travel companions, who will visit your site to view or edit the plan. This creates organic repeat traffic without paid ads.

<Tip>
  Follow up within 24 hours of lead capture. Guests who save an itinerary are actively planning a trip, so timely outreach significantly improves conversion.
</Tip>

## Understand what guests want

Click any lead in your admin feed to view the full generated itinerary. This shows you exactly which tours, dates, and experiences the guest is interested in. Use this insight to tailor your follow-up message or adjust your inventory to match demand.
