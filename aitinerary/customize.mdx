AItinerary is built to feel like an extension of your brand, not a generic chatbot. You control where it operates, how it speaks, and which tours it recommends first. Customization takes only a few minutes and ensures every guest interaction reinforces your positioning.

## Set your destination scope

Choose whether AItinerary focuses on a single city or covers multiple regions. This controls the geographic knowledge the AI uses when building itineraries.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to AItinerary > Settings">
    Open the customization panel from your Tripbone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select destination mode">
    Choose **Single City** if you operate in one location, or **Multi-Region** if you cover a broader area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter destination names">
    List the cities or regions you serve. The AI will only recommend experiences within these boundaries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adjust the AI's brand voice

The tone of the itinerary interview and output should match your brand. AItinerary supports three preset voice profiles:

| Voice | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Adventurous | Active tours, outdoor experiences, thrill-seeking guests |
| Luxurious | Private tours, premium packages, high-budget travelers |
| Family-Friendly | Group tours, kid-friendly activities, relaxed pacing |

Select a voice in **AItinerary \> Settings \> Brand Voice**. The AI will adjust its word choice, pacing suggestions, and activity mix to match.

## How inventory matching works

Your tours always appear first in every generated itinerary. AItinerary scans your live inventory and prioritizes:

1. Your own published tours that match the guest's dates and interests
2. Your own tours with available slots
3. Complementary local recommendations (if enabled) to fill gaps

You can disable third-party recommendations entirely so every plan is 100% your inventory.

## Multi-destination ready setup

If you operate across multiple cities or countries, enable **Multi-Region** mode and list each destination. The AI will build inter-city itineraries, suggest travel days, and match your tours across the entire route. This is ideal for operators running regional networks or multi-day tour packages.

<Note>
  Changes to destination scope or brand voice apply immediately to all new itinerary sessions. Existing saved itineraries are not retroactively updated.
</Note>
