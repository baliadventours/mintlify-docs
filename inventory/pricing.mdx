Tripbone's pricing engine lets you build flexible rate structures that match how real travelers book: families with children, solo adventurers, couples, and large groups. You can set age-based tiers, scale prices by party size, offer optional add-ons, and display prices in the currency your guests expect. There are zero booking fees on every transaction, so the price you set is the revenue you keep.

## Open the Pricing panel

1. From the left sidebar, choose **Inventory**.
2. Click a tour name to open its detail page.
3. Select the **Pricing** tab.

## Configure age-based tiers

Most tours need at least an Adult rate. You can add Child and Infant tiers when your experience suits families.

1. Under **Base Pricing**, toggle on the tiers you need:
   - **Adult** (required)
   - **Child**
   - **Infant**
2. Enter a price for each tier.
3. Set the **Age ranges** that define each tier (for example, Child: 3 to 12 years, Infant: 0 to 2 years).
4. Click **Save**.

Guests entering passenger details during checkout see the correct tier applied automatically based on birth date or age input.

## Add quantity-based group pricing

You can reward larger parties with per-person discounts that scale automatically as the group grows.

1. In the **Group Pricing** section, click **Add Rule**.
2. Define a quantity range and the per-person price for that bracket.

| Tier | Quantity range | Per-person price | Example use case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo | 1 guest | \$120 | Single traveler premium |
| Couple | 2 guests | \$110 each | Small pair discount |
| Group | 3 to 6 guests | \$95 each | Friends or family rate |
| Large group | 7\+ guests | \$85 each | Corporate or reunion rate |

3. Toggle **Stack with age tiers** if the group discount should apply on top of Adult/Child/Infant rates, or leave it off if the group price replaces all tier logic.
4. Click **Save**.

## Create revenue-boosting add-ons

Add-ons let you increase average order value without raising the base tour price.

1. Under **Add-ons**, click **Create Add-on**.
2. Enter the add-on name, description, and price.
3. Choose whether it is **Per person** or **Per booking**.
4. Toggle **Require selection** only if the add-on is mandatory (for example, a national park entry fee).

Common add-ons that perform well:

- GoPro rental or photo package
- Premium lunch or tasting menu upgrade
- Private transport or hotel pickup
- Extended route or extra stop

Guests see add-ons as optional checkboxes during checkout.

## Enable multi-currency display

If you market to international travelers, you can show prices in their home currency.

1. In **Regional Settings** (from the tour Pricing tab or global Store Setup), toggle **Multi-currency**.
2. Select the currencies you want to support.
3. Tripbone fetches live exchange rates and displays the converted price next to your base currency.

The transaction still settles in your default currency; the display currency is for guest convenience.
