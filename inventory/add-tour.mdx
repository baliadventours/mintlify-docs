Adding a tour to Tripbone turns your experience into a bookable product that guests can discover, browse, and reserve directly on your storefront. A complete listing includes photos, an AI-generated description, a mapped meeting point, and live availability so bookings flow in automatically.

## Before you start

- Your [store setup](/getting-started/setup) should be complete (logo, currency, domain).
- Have at least 3 to 5 high-quality tour photos ready (JPG or PNG, under 5 MB each).
- Know the tour meeting point address or coordinates.

## Create a new tour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inventory panel and click 'Add Tour'">
    From the left sidebar, choose **Inventory**, then click the **Add Tour** button in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter basic details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Tour title** — the name guests see (e.g., "Sunrise Kayak & Snorkel Adventure")
    - **Category** — select from your catalog (e.g., Water Sports, Culture, Food & Drink)
    - **Zone / Destination** — the city, region, or neighborhood where the tour operates
    - **Duration** — approximate runtime in hours and minutes
    - **Difficulty / Fitness level** — optional, but helpful for guest expectations Click **Save Draft** after each section to preserve progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload photos to the gallery">
    In the **Gallery** tab, drag and drop your images. The first image becomes the cover photo on your storefront and in search results. Add a short **Alt text** for each image to improve accessibility and SEO. Click **Reorder** if you want to change the sequence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a meeting point via OpenStreetMap">
    In the **Meeting Point** section, type the address or landmark name. Tripbone uses OpenStreetMap to suggest locations. Click the correct result to drop a pin. Guests see a map on the tour page and can open directions in their preferred navigation app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the AI Tour Architect to generate description and itinerary">
    Click **Generate with AI** in the **Description** and **Itinerary** fields. Tripbone's Google Gemini integration writes a compelling summary and a step-by-step day plan based on your title, category, and duration. Review the output, edit as needed, and click **Accept**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set capacity and time slots">
    Under **Availability**, enter the **Maximum guests per departure** (e.g., 12). Then add your default time slots (e.g., 08:00, 14:00). You can refine these later in the Availability module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    When every section is complete, toggle the status from **Draft** to **Published** at the top of the page. The tour appears on your storefront immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you run similar tours with only small differences (for example, a morning vs sunset version of the same route), use the **Clone Engine**. Open any existing tour, click **Clone**, adjust the title, time slots, and photos, and publish in seconds.
</Tip>

## After publishing

Once live, your tour page includes:

- A **Book Now** button connected to real-time availability
- An **AI itinerary preview** if you used the AI Tour Architect
- Automatic SEO meta tags generated from your title and description
- A shareable link you can paste into Instagram, TikTok, or email campaigns
