The Inventory module is where you build, organize, and control every experience you sell. From a single dashboard you can add new tours, adjust pricing across age tiers and group sizes, manage daily time slots, and assign vendors or guides to specific products. Changes update in real time, so your storefront and booking calendar always stay in sync.

## What Inventory covers

- **Tour catalog** — every published and draft tour, experience, or activity you offer
- **Pricing** — per-person tiers (Adult, Child, Infant), group discounts, and optional add-ons
- **Availability** — daily or multi-slot schedules, seasonal capacity, and blackout dates
- **Vendors** — external suppliers or freelance guides linked to specific tours for commission tracking

## Inventory at a glance

When you open the Inventory panel, you see:

- **Total tours** — published vs draft count
- **Live capacity** — seats available today across all tours
- **Next 7-day occupancy** — a quick visual of how full your schedule is running
- **Pending vendor assignments** — tours that still need a guide or supplier linked

Use the search bar and filters (category, destination, vendor, status) to find any tour instantly, even with hundreds of listings.
