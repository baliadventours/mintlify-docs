Availability management in Tripbone keeps your calendar accurate without constant manual updates. You define when a tour runs, how many seats are open per departure, and how far in advance guests must book. The live occupancy engine counts confirmed reservations in real time and closes a slot automatically when it hits capacity, so you never accidentally overbook.

## Open the Availability panel

1. From the left sidebar, choose **Inventory**.
2. Click a tour name to open its detail page.
3. Select the **Availability** tab.

## Choose a schedule type

Tripbone supports two ways to structure your calendar.

### Daily schedule

Use this when a tour runs once per day at a fixed time, such as a full-day island cruise that departs every morning at 09:00.

1. Under **Schedule Type**, select **Daily**.
2. Pick the default **Departure time**.
3. Choose the **Days of the week** the tour operates (for example, Tuesday through Sunday, closed Monday).
4. Set the **Default capacity** (maximum guests per departure).
5. Click **Save**.

### Multi-slot schedule

Use this when a tour runs several times a day, such as a city walking tour with morning, afternoon, and evening departures.

1. Under **Schedule Type**, select **Multi-slot**.
2. Click **Add Slot** for each departure time you offer (for example, Sunrise 06:00, Midday 12:00, Sunset 17:30).
3. Assign a **Capacity** and optional **Slot label** to each time.
4. Choose the **Days of the week** each slot runs.
5. Click **Save**.

Guests browsing your storefront see every open slot and can pick the one that fits their itinerary.

## Block dates in bulk

When you need to close multiple days at once (holidays, maintenance, guide vacations), use the bulk blocker instead of editing each day individually.

1. Click **Block Dates** in the Availability tab.
2. Select a **Date range** on the calendar.
3. Choose whether to block **All slots** or only specific time slots.
4. Add an optional **Reason** (visible internally to your team).
5. Click **Apply Block**.

Blocked dates appear grayed out on the public calendar and cannot be booked.

## Configure automatic booking cutoff

A cutoff window prevents last-minute reservations that you do not have time to prepare for.

1. Under **Booking Rules**, find **Cutoff window**.
2. Enter a value and unit (for example, **2 hours**, **24 hours**, or **3 days** before departure).
3. Click **Save**.

Once the cutoff passes, the slot is automatically hidden from the storefront and unavailable for new bookings.

<Warning>
  Always set a cutoff window. Without one, a guest could book five minutes before departure, leaving you no time to assign a guide, prepare equipment, or confirm logistics.
</Warning>

## Adjust seasonal capacity

If your tour runs larger groups in peak season and smaller, intimate groups in low season, create seasonal capacity rules.

1. Click **Add Season** under **Capacity Rules**.
2. Define the **Date range** (for example, June 1 to August 31).
3. Set the **Capacity override** for that period.
4. Click **Save**.

The override applies only within the season range; outside those dates, the default capacity resumes automatically.

## Monitor live occupancy

At the top of the Availability tab, the **Live Occupancy** bar shows:

- **Total seats** today across all slots for this tour
- **Confirmed bookings**
- **Remaining availability**
- **Waitlist count** (if you enabled waitlists in Settings)

If a slot is at risk of selling out, the bar turns amber. When fully booked, it turns red and the slot closes to new reservations.
