## Why Tripbone?

- **0% booking fees**: Keep every dollar from every reservation. Tripbone does not charge commissions or per-booking fees.
- **Google Gemini AI built-in**: Generate tour descriptions, blog posts, SEO meta tags, and AI itineraries automatically.
- **One-time price, no monthly fees**: Pay once and own the platform. No recurring subscriptions.
- **Own your customer data**: Your guest lists, booking history, and leads belong to you, not a marketplace.
- **Mobile-first storefront**: Your store looks great and books smoothly on any phone, tablet, or desktop.
